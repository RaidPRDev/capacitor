Rocuronium:
Intravenous nondepolarizing neuromuscular blocking agent
ECMO Dose Adjustment:
NONE
Renal Impairment Dose Adjustment*:
YES—a decreased dose may be warranted in some patients.
CRRT Dose Adjustment:
NONE
Hepatic Impairment Dose Adjustment*:
YES—a decreased dose may be warranted in some patients.
Adult starting dose^:
Adult Emergency Dose: 1 mg/kg IV x 1
Adult Intermittent: 0.5–1 mg/kg IV every 30–60 min
Adult Continuous Infusion: 3–5 mcg/kg/min
Pediatric starting dose^:
Pediatric Emergency Dose: 1 mg/kg IV x 1
Pediatric Intermittent: 0.5–1 mg/kg IV every 30–60 min
Pediatric Continuous infusion: 5–7 mcg/kg/min
Neonatal starting dose^:
Neonatal Emergency Dose: 1 mg/kg IV x 1
Neonatal Intermittent: 0.5–1 mg/kg IV every 30–60 min
Neonatal Continuous Infusion: 5–7 mcg/kg/min
*Dose adjustment will be dependent on acuity and severity of impairment.
^Always use the lowest effect dose to achieve an objective endpoint (e.g., train-of-four, ventilator synchrony) and ensure adequate pain control and deep sedation prior to and during administration of neuromuscular blocking agents.
