The Anticoagulation Ladder
  Bivalirudin Heparin
     

Level 0

(Off)

 0 mg/kg/hr 0 u/kg/hr

Level 1

(Fixed dose, no titration)

 0.03–0.05 mg/kg/hr 10 u/kg/hr

Level 2

(Low range)

 PTT 50s–70s ACT 160s–180s

Level 3

(Normal range)

 PTT 60s–80s ACT 180s–200s
Notes:
  • Post-procedure: Start at 0 for 4–6 hours or longer to ensure the patient is not bleeding (nitric oxide delivered via the ECMO circuit (sNO)—if used—can be continued throughout).
  • Then, start at Level 1 for 6 hours or longer to ensure the patient is not bleeding
  • Then, move to Level 2 for 24 hours
  • Then, Level 3 thereafter.
  • If there is bleeding, go down one level until the bleeding stops for 4–6 hours, then go back up.
Considerations:
  • Is a surgery or other intervention possible to stop the bleeding?
  • Consider adding an antifibrinolytic in bleeding patients
    • Epsilon-Amino-Caproic Acid 100 mg/kg load over 30–60 min then 33.3 mg/kg/hr infusion
    • Tranexamic Acid
      • IV 4 mg/Kg load then 1 mg/kg/hr
      • Neb <25 kg 250 mg/2.5 ml, >25 kg 500 mg/5 ml Q6
  • Optimize platelet count, fibrinogen and INR if bleeding
  • Consider prothrombin complex concentrate (PCC) at 25 iu/kg and repeat up to 4 times if bleeding severely*
  • Carefully consider Novo7 30 mcg/kg and repeat 3 times if bleeding is life threatening and other steps have been ineffective*
* Ensure there is a backup circuit at the bedside if using PCC or Novo7
