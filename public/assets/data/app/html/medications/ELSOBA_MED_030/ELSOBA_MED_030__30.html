Meropenem/vaborbactam:
Intravenous carbapenem
ECMO Dose Adjustment:
NONE
Renal Impairment Dose Adjustment*:
YES—a decreased dose may be warranted in some patients
CRRT Dose Adjustment:
YES—the dose may need to be increased relative to the renal impairment dose
Hepatic Impairment Dose Adjustment:
NONE
Adult Dose:
4 g IV every 8 hours
Pediatric Dose:
2 to < 6 years of age: 120 mg/kg (60 mg/kg meropenem, 60 mg/kg vaborbactam) IV over 3 hours every 8 hours
≥ 6 years of age: 80 mg/kg (40 mg/kg meropenem, 40 mg/kg vaborbactam) IV over 3 hours every 8 hours; maximum 4 g (2 g meropenem, 2 g vaborbactam)
Neonatal Dose:
No information. Use alternative antimicrobial
*Dose adjustment will be dependent on acuity and severity of impairment
