Azithromycin:
Intravenous macrolide
ECMO Dose Adjustment:
NONE
Renal Impairment Dose Adjustment:
NONE
CRRT Dose Adjustment:
NONE
Hepatic Impairment Dose Adjustment:
NONE
Adult Dose:
500 mg IV every 24 hours
Pediatric Dose:
10 mg/kg/dose (maximum 500 mg) IV every 24 hours for 1–2 doses, followed by 5 mg/kg/dose (maximum 250 mg) IV every 24 hours to complete a 5-day course
Neonatal Dose:
pertussis: 10 mg/kg/dose IV every 24 hours
