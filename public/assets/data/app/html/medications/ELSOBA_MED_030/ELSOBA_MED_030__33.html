Moxifloxacin:
Intravenous fluoroquinolone
ECMO Dose Adjustment:
NONE
Renal Impairment Dose Adjustment:
NONE
CRRT Dose Adjustment:
NONE
Hepatic Impairment Dose Adjustment:
NONE
Adult Dose:
400 mg IV every 24 hours
Pediatric Dose:
≥ 3 months to < 2 years of age: 6 mg/kg/dose IV every 12 hours; maximum dose 200 mg/dose
2 to < 6 years of age: 5 mg/kg/dose IV every 12 hours; maximum dose 200 mg/dose
6 to < 12 years of age: 4 mg/kg/dose IV every 12 hours; maximum dose: 200 mg/dose
≥ 12 years of age: < 45 kg: 4 mg/kg/dose IV every 12 hours; maximum dose: 200 mg/dose
  • ≥ 45 kg: 400 mg IV every 24 hours
Neonatal Dose:
Gestational Age 32 to 37 weeks: 5 mg/kg/dose IV every 24 hours
Gestational Age > 37 weeks: 10 mg/kg/dose IV every 24 hours
