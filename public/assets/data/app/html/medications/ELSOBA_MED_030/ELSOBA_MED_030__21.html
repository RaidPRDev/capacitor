Doxycycline:
Intravenous tetracycline derivative
ECMO Dose Adjustment:
NONE. Consider repeat load if on therapy when cannulated
Renal Impairment Dose Adjustment:
NONE
CRRT Dose Adjustment:
NONE
Hepatic Impairment Dose Adjustment:
NONE
Adult Dose:
200 mg IV x1, followed by 100 mg IV every 12 hours
Pediatric Dose:
2.2 mg/kg/dose (max dose 100 mg) IV every 12 hours
Neonatal Dose:
Limited data. Anthrax: 2.2 mg/kg/dose IV every 12 hours
