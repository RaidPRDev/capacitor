Minocycline:
Intravenous tetracycline derivative
ECMO Dose Adjustment:
NONE
Renal Impairment Dose Adjustment:
NONE
CRRT Dose Adjustment:
NONE
Hepatic Impairment Dose Adjustment:
NONE
Adult Dose:
200 mg IV every 12 hours
Pediatric Dose:
Do not use in children < 8 years of age unless the expected benefits of therapy outweigh the risks.
> 8 years of age: initial 4 mg/kg/dose (max 200 mg) IV, followed by 2 mg/kg/dose (max 100 mg/dose) IV every 12 hours
Neonatal Dose:
No data. Use alternate antimicrobial
