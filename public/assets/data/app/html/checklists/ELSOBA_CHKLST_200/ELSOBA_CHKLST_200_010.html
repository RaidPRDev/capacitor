• Wide range of short and long-term disabilities including motor impairment and cognitive delays, behavioral abnormalities, and lower quality of life scores compared to healthy and non-ECMO patients.
  • There is currently no consensus about the most reliable and effective way to provide neuromonitoring care for patients on ECMO.
    • Not all modalities are widely available.
    • Many studies looking at neuromonitoring are largely based on single center experience.
    • Many of these modalities determine injury after it has already occurred and do not in and of themselves predict neurologic risk.
    • ELSO has put out a neuromonitoring guideline for pediatric and neonatal patients based on expert consensus.
