• ICU team confirms the ECMO circuit preservation plan with the surgical team.
  • Prep and drape so the ECMO cannulas and tubing are in the surgical field:
    • Dressings off the cannulation sites; sutures secure
    • Drapes loose where the tubes exit the field, so they can be withdrawn later
    • Single site (neck or groin): one exit point for the tubing. Multisite: each line free to be withdrawn (one drape under and one over the tubing, nothing fixed to the tubing)
  • Introduce the sterile portion of the CPB lines to the sterile field, keeping 2 clamps on the non-sterile portions.
  • Clamp the drainage / return CPB lines to the desired tubing length and cut.
  • Insert the straight connectors on the drainage / return CPB lines.
  • Start ACT monitoring and anticoagulation management per the institutional CPB protocol.
