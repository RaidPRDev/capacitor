Critical: Lead surgeon asks the perfusionist for a CPB pump STOP.
  • Verbal confirm: Perfusionist stops the CPB pump.
  • Clamp the venous and arterial CPB lines close to the cannulas.
  • Place another clamp at 10–15 cm and cut between the two clamps.
  • Connect the drainage / return ECMO lines using an air-free technique.
Critical: Verify that no air is present.
Critical: Unclamp the ECMO lines and ask the perfusionist to START the ECMO pump.
  • Remove the clamp on the ECMO drainage line, start the ECMO pump and, when safe, remove the clamp on the return side.
  • Verbal confirm: Confirm ECMO ON.
