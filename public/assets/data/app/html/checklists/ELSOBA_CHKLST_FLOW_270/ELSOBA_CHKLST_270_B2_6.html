• ECMO to zero RPM and sweep off as soon as ECMO is clamped off at the field.
  • Join the tubing ends and recirculate:
    • Single-site cannulation: join the tubing ends in the field with a standard airless connection technique; remove the field clamps, pull the tubing back through the drapes, hang a sash on the circuit, move the circuit away from the patient and recirculate
    • Multisite cannulation: cover both tubing ends with a finger cut from a surgical glove, tied on with a silk ligature; clamp both tubes outside the field and remove the field clamps; pull the tubing back and move the circuit away from the patient; with an aseptic, airless technique, join the ends with a connector to recirculate; hang the sash on the circuit
  • Remove the clamps and set the RPM for recirculation flow:
    • Neonatal circuit: 300–500 ml/min
    • Pediatric circuit: 500–1000 ml/min
    • Adult circuit: 1–2 L/min
    • Neonatal circuit with an adult membrane lung and a bridge: about 1 L/min through the bridge and 300 ml/min around the sash
  • Restart the heater and set it to 36.5 °C.
  • Leave the sweep gas off; if the blood becomes noticeably dark, flash the sweep until it is red, then turn it off again.
  • Check the circuit ACT hourly:
    • Stop the pump, clamp downstream of the sample pigtail, connect the sample syringe to the pigtail and open the stopcock to the circuit
    • Connect a saline syringe to a distant stopcock and inject saline into the circuit as you aspirate the sample
    • Close the stopcocks and restart the pump
