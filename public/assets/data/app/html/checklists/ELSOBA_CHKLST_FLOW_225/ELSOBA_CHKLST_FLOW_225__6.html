• Personnel roles allocated.
  • Consent (if feasible).
  • Plan the anticoagulation bolus, if required and not contraindicated:
    • Medication
    • Dose
    • Timing
  • Secure IV access for the procedure, separate from the sites of ECMO cannulation.
  • Medications:
    • Sedation and analgesia
    • Volume
    • Blood products
    • Emergency drugs, for example:
      • Epinephrine (code dose and spritzer)
      • Calcium (may be needed for hypotension at ECMO initiation)
      • Bicarbonate
  • Position the patient:
    • Is the electrocautery pad needed / on?
    • Are pressure points padded?
    • Are lines and tubes secured and positioned well?
  • Set up imaging:
    • Ultrasound
    • X-ray screening: is the bed x-ray compatible, lead aprons, radiation safety of those nearby
