• Check the circuit:
    • Drainage and return tubing sizes
    • Membrane lung rated blood flow
    • Connectors to fit the cannulas
    • Connectors / stopcocks for sampling and adjunctive therapies
    • Priming volume (clear / blood)
  • Match cannulas to vessels:
    • Check vessel size against cannula size charts and maximum flow rate
    • Choose a cannula large enough to provide the expected flow and small enough to fit the vessel
    • Bring 2 × intended cannulas (with guidewires connected, if used), plus one size larger and one size smaller
    Maximum cannula size (Fr) = vessel diameter (mm) × 3
  • Match guidewires (if used) and connectors:
    • Guidewire size and length vs. the access to be wired out and the cannula to be inserted
    • Connectors vs. circuit tubing diameter
    Guidewires are not always used, particularly in neonates and children.
  • Blood products available for cannulation.
